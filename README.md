# Dr. PLACK Sokhit

**Lecturer in Transport Engineering · Institute of Technology of Cambodia (ITC), Phnom Penh**

I teach and research transport planning, with a focus on walkability, traffic-related air pollution, air transport, urban mobility and digital twins of transport and logistics infrastructure in Cambodia. I build practical web tools that turn engineering models and data into something planners, operators and students can use.

---

### Research and practice areas

- **Walkability and pedestrians:** walkability indices, sidewalk quality and pedestrian perceptions in Phnom Penh
- **Traffic and air quality:** pedestrian exposure to traffic pollution and motorcycle emissions
- **Air transport planning and management:** airport capacity and level of service, airline planning, safety and sustainable aviation
- **Digital twins:** 3D models of logistics parks, school zones and campuses, linked to live or sample data
- **Smart mobility:** road safety around schools, mobility apps and data for Phnom Penh
- **Data analysis and BIM:** turning spreadsheets and models into dashboards and decisions
